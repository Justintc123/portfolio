## Selected projects

### Nova — A local AI assistant
**Role:** Independent developer  
**Stage:** Working prototype, ongoing development

Nova started with a simple idea: I wanted an assistant that could do useful things on my own computer and connect with electronics projects.

The current version integrates **Gemma2 2B through Ollama** for local text conversations. It includes conversation storage, notes, tasks, project information, and search in local documentation. It also includes tools for working with files.

I’ve also worked on voice interaction, computer vision, and hardware connections, including experiments with ESP32 boards and a small robotic arm. These parts are at different stages of development and testing.

**Next steps:** Improve reliability and measure memory use, speed, and response quality across different computers, especially machines with limited resources.

**Tools and topics:** Python, Ollama, local language models, memory storage, voice interfaces, computer vision, microcontrollers.

Nova integrates existing AI models; I did not train its underlying language model. Running the complete assistant within 2 GB is a development goal, not a verified result.

### Nexus — Technology for my school
**Role:** Team leader  
**Team:** Six students  
**Stage:** Prototype development

I lead a team of six students developing a school technology system that connects software with classroom hardware.

Our team received **municipal funding** to develop the project. This is one of the achievements I’m most proud of: an idea became a project with a team and real support behind it.

The work includes a local server, backend services, an Android application, and an RFID attendance prototype. Our classroom hardware combines a card reader with a touchscreen interface. The classroom prototype is scoped to **one classroom** and is still being developed.

**My contribution:** Leading the team, helping connect the software and hardware work, and presenting the project.

**Tools and topics:** Python, FastAPI, Linux, Android/Java, RFID, ESP32, STM32, touchscreen interfaces, local networking.

### Robotics and electronics experiments
**Role:** Student builder  
**Stage:** Hands-on learning and prototypes

Alongside Nova and Nexus, I build smaller projects to learn more about electronics and robotics. These include a servo-driven robotic arm, ESP32 devices, and interfaces for embedded screens.

One recent challenge has been improving a small 3D-printed arm. Its original PLA structure and small servos limited what it could support. I’m exploring changes to its size, joints, and motors, and learning how mechanical choices affect the electronics and control software.
